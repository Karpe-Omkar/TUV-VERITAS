Our Story

Certification shouldn't feel like a black box.

TUV Veritas was established in Pune with a simple goal: make ISO and product certification understandable and achievable for organizations of every size — not just large enterprises with dedicated compliance teams.

We work with manufacturers, exporters, technology companies, and service providers who need their quality, environmental, or safety management systems independently verified — and who need that verification to be respected by customers and regulators well beyond India's borders.

Our approach combines structured, standards-based assessment with plain-language guidance, so that certification becomes a tool your team actually understands and sustains, not a document that sits in a drawer.
