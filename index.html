How Certification Works

A clear path from enquiry to certificate

Every engagement follows the same disciplined sequence, so you always know what happens next.

1

Enquiry & Scoping

We understand your business, products and target markets to define the right certification scope.

2

Documentation Review

Your existing processes and records are reviewed against the applicable standard's requirements.

3

Stage 1 Audit

A readiness review confirms your management system is prepared for full assessment.

4

Stage 2 Audit

Auditors assess implementation and effectiveness on-site or remotely against every clause.

5

Certification & Surveillance

Once approved, your certificate is issued, with ongoing surveillance audits to maintain it.
